CRITICAL"Connect your phone" is an OS-assisted import, not browser-over-USB. Browsers cannot read a phone's photo library over USB. The 3 steps are: 1) Plug phone into your PC via USB · 2) Your computer opens the phone (Windows: This PC → phone → DCIM; Mac: Photos / Image Capture, or Android File Transfer) · 3) Select those files here.

Purpose

Remove the "how do I get phone footage onto my computer?" wall — three parallel paths, phone flow guided step-by-step.

Key interactions

  • Tabs: drag-drop · file/folder picker · connect phone
  • Chunked, resumable uploads with per-file progress
  • Thumbnails populate as files land
  • Continue enabled once ≥1 clip uploaded
OS-assisted, not USB-to-browserResumable / chunked
