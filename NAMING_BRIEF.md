# Naming Brief — Auto Video-Maker SaaS

*A brief for an AI (or person) to generate brandable name + domain ideas. Everything you
need is here; you don't need outside context.*

---

## 1. What the product is

A mobile-first SaaS (iPhone + Android, plus a web companion later). The user:

1. Selects photos **and** videos from their phone's gallery.
2. Uploads them to the platform.
3. The platform **automatically stitches them into a polished highlight video and adds
   music** — no manual editing required.

Think "drop in your memories, get back a ready-to-post music video." The heavy editing and
rendering happens in the **cloud**, not on the phone (that's the core SaaS advantage).

**One-liner:** *Turn the photos and videos already on your phone into a polished,
music-driven video in under a minute — without editing anything yourself.*

## 2. What makes it distinct (brand feeling to evoke)

- **Effortless / automatic** — the magic is that AI does the editing for you.
- **Music-driven** — auto-synced music is a headline feature (beat-matched cuts).
- **Memories → shareable moments** — emotional, personal, joyful.
- **Fast** — quick, snappy, "instant."
- **Many pieces → one** — dozens of clips become a single beautiful video.

A great name can lean on any of these: motion, music/rhythm, memory, speed, or the
"many-become-one" idea.

## 3. Target users

Primary (launch): everyday people making **travel / event / birthday / wedding recap
reels**. Later: real-estate agents, small businesses (product promos), creators.

## 4. Naming criteria (hard requirements)

1. **Short** — ideally 5–8 letters, two syllables, one or two at most.
2. **Passes the "say it, spell it" test** — if said out loud, a stranger types it correctly
   on the first try. **This is the #1 rule.**
3. **`.com` must be actually available** (verify against a registry — see §7).
4. **Brandable** — a coined/invented word is fine and often better than a real word.
5. **No trademark collision** — avoid names close to known brands (do a USPTO check on
   finalists).
6. **Global-friendly** — easy to pronounce for non-English speakers; no unfortunate meanings
   in major languages.

### Hard NO's (learned the hard way)
- ❌ **No deliberately misspelled real words** with doubled vowels/consonants
  (e.g. `viibeoo`, `viibbee`). They're "available" only because they're unspellable, and
  they leak traffic to the correctly-spelled competitor forever.
- ❌ No awkward consonant clusters that trip the tongue.
- ❌ No hyphens or numbers.

## 5. Directions already explored (and the reality)

- **"Vid-" and "Reel-" real words / simple combos** → essentially all taken.
- **Coined "vibe/video" words** (Vibeo, Reelay, Vibato, Reelody, Memoreel…) → the good clean
  ones are taken.
- **Real animal / bird / prehistoric words** (lyrebird, quetzal, narwhal, mammoth, raptor,
  starling, corvo, vireo…) → **100% taken**. Do not suggest bare animal words; they're gone.
- **Winning pattern so far:** *coined/invented words*, or a theme word fused with an unusual
  ending. Availability lives in invented territory, not dictionary territory.

Good metaphors worth mining (all evoke "many clips → one video"): a **murmuration** of
starlings, a **flock**, **weaving** threads, a **montage**, **rhythm/tempo**, **flight**.

`vidsona.com` · `murmuur.com` · `flocka.com` · `mammora.com` · `mammoto.com` ·
`vibraptor.com` · `vibquetzal.com`

Current front-runners in the user's mind: **vibdanza**, **murmuur**, **flocka**.

## 7. What to deliver

Generate **~25–40 candidate names**. For each, provide:

| Name | Meaning / metaphor | Why it fits | `.com` status |
|------|--------------------|-------------|---------------|

**You MUST verify `.com` availability, not guess.** Reliable free method — query the
registry via RDAP and treat a 404 as available, a 200 (with registration data) as taken:

```
https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com
```

Then present a final ranked shortlist of the **5 best names that are actually available**,
with your single top recommendation and a one-line rationale. If the user wants something
short and clean and the `.com` is gone, also check `.app` and `.co` and say so.
